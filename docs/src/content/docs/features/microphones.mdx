---
title: Microphones
description: What each microphone setting does and how to tune for your room.
---

Find the microphone settings in Home Assistant on the device's **microphone** sub-device or in
the device's [web page](/use/web/). They're also in the
[EchoLocal card](/start/integration/#the-card)'s Microphone panel. They stay on
the device: [config sync](/use/configuration/#config-sync) doesn't copy them.

| Setting | Entity | What it does | Range | Default |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Microphone mixing** | `select.<device>_microphone_microphone_mixing` | How the microphones are combined. **Center mic** uses one; on the Echo Dot's seven-microphone array, **Delay and sum**, and **Beamformer** where the Echo's own beamformer tuning is present, aim at whoever is speaking | Center mic, Delay and sum, Beamformer | Center mic |
| **Microphone gain** | `number.<device>_microphone_microphone_gain` | Amplification before anything else | 0–59 dB | 20 dB |
| **Microphone leveling** | `switch.<device>_microphone_microphone_leveling` | Keeps your voice at a steady level whether you're near or across the room | On, Off | On |
| **Microphone echo cancellation** | `switch.<device>_microphone_microphone_echo_cancellation` | Removes what the speaker is playing from what the microphones hear, so the wake word works while music plays | On, Off | On |
| **Room sensitivity** | `number.<device>_microphone_room_sensitivity` | How much louder than the room's background noise a sound has to be before the ring or the listening visual reacts | 4–20 dB | 8 dB |
| **Mute sound** | `select.<device>_microphone_mute_sound` | The sound when the mute button is pressed | Chimes, Mute tones, custom tones | Mute tones |

The Echo Shows offer only **Center mic** mixing.

The **Room level** (`room_level`) and **Room floor** (`room_floor`) sensors report the current sound
level and background noise floor.

## Tuning

- **The wake word misses you from across the room.** Make the wake word easier to trigger first:
  lower **Wake word sensitivity** in Home Assistant, or raise its slider on the screen; then raise **Microphone gain** a few dB at a time. Keep **Microphone
  leveling** on.
- **It wakes without a wake word, or on the TV.** Make the wake word harder to trigger: raise **Wake word sensitivity** in Home Assistant, or lower
  its slider on the screen. Keep **Microphone echo cancellation** on so the device doesn't hear its own music.
- **It cuts you off, or keeps listening too long.** Adjust **Max listening time** to change the
  time limit. **Room sensitivity** changes the visual response, not when listening ends.
- **Voices sound distorted in the recordings.** Lower **Microphone gain**.

## What the visuals hear

The screen saver's visuals listen to the same microphone signal, after echo cancellation, so music
from the speaker barely moves them while your voice does. **Visualizer lift** (`visualizer_lift`,
0–40 dB) adds gain for the visuals only, without changing what the wake word hears.
