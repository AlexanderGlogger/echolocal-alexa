Settings are saved on the device. Changes made on the screen, in a browser or in Home Assistant
are reflected in the other interfaces.

| Where | What's there |
|---|---|
| **On an Echo Show**: the gear in the dock | Nearly everything, grouped the way a phone's settings are. [Settings on the screen](/use/settings/) |
| **On the EchoLocal card** | The same settings, laid out by area, with a **?** beside each that explains it. [The EchoLocal integration](/start/integration/#the-card) |
| **In a browser**: the device's own web page | Every setting, plus alarms, calls, broadcasts and troubleshooting. [In a browser](/use/web/) |
| **In Home Assistant** | An entity for each setting, on the device page and usable in dashboards and automations, including settings not on the screen. [Settings in Home Assistant](/use/entities/) |

The Echo Dot has no screen. Use its [web page](/use/web/), Home Assistant or the EchoLocal card
to change its settings.

## Finding a setting

1. Look it up on [Settings on the screen](/use/settings/). Each row there names its Home Assistant
   entity.
2. If it isn't there, it's on [Settings in Home Assistant](/use/entities/).
3. In Home Assistant, open **Settings → Devices & services → ESPHome**, pick the device, and look
   under the sub-device the setting belongs to ([which one](/start/home-assistant/#in-home-assistant)).

## Settings that stay on one device

Config sync excludes these device-local settings:

- Brightness, auto brightness and display size
- Microphone tuning: gain, leveling, noise and echo cancellation, sensitivity
- Camera picture and stream settings
- Bluetooth scanning
- Home control: which tabs are on, the entities picked for them, and how they're grouped
- The intercom's auto-answer lists: panels and users
- The Cameras app's Home Assistant URL
- Whether Home Assistant's API adoption is allowed

The device's name and Wi‑Fi aren't settings, so they aren't copied either.

## Config sync

Copy shared settings from one device to another.
It works between Echo Shows, and with Lenovo Smart Displays running LANovo, on the same network;
settings the other device doesn't have are skipped. The Echo Dot doesn't take part in this
screen-to-screen flow; use the integration's [Fleet tab](/start/integration/#fleet) to copy settings
to or from a Dot.

<Steps>

1. On one device, open **Settings → System → Config sync**.

2. Find the other device, then tap its upload arrow to send this one's settings to it, or its
   download arrow to fetch its settings.

3. Enter the six-digit code shown on the other device.

</Steps>

The receiving device reports how many settings it applied.
